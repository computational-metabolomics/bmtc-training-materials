# Birmingham Metabolomics Training Centre (BMTC) - Training Materials

This repository contains training materials for the [BMTC](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/facilities/metabolomics-training-centre) at the [University of Birmingham](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/).

</div>


## About BMTC

The [Birmingham Metabolomics Training Centre (BMTC)](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/facilities/metabolomics-training-centre) provides training to the metabolomics community in both analytical and computational methods
We provide face-to-face and online metabolomics courses, from introductory courses for researchers new to metabolomics, through advanced hands-on learning opportunities. Our partnership with the [Phenome Centre Birmingham](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/research/centres-institutes/phenome-centre-birmingham) enables us to provide vocational training courses in metabolomics, providing expert trainer assistance and guidance for our delegates.

If you are interested in metabolomics then we have a course suitable for you, regardless of sector, career stage, or geographical location. Have a look on our [Course List page](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/facilities/metabolomics-training-centre/course-list) today to get started.


## Repository Contents

### Pre-Course Materials

This repository currently contains pre-course materials designed to help participants prepare for BMTC training courses. These materials provide foundational knowledge and ensure all participants have the necessary background before attending our workshops.

📂 **[Browse the pre-course materials](https://computational-metabolomics.github.io/bmtc-training-materials/)**

## Using These Materials


### For Course Participants

If you're registered for a BMTC course, please review the pre-course materials before your training begins. Your course instructors will provide specific guidance on which materials are relevant for your particular course.

### For Independent Learners

These materials are openly available for anyone interested in learning about metabolomics. While they're designed to complement our structured training courses, they can also serve as standalone learning resources.

## Contributing

We welcome contributions to improve these training materials. Contributions are particularly welcome for:
- Fixing typos, broken links, or formatting issues
- Clarifying instructions or explanations
- Updating outdated information
- Reporting issues or bugs in code examples

To contribute:
1. Fork this repository
2. Create a new branch for your changes
3. Submit a pull request with a clear description of your improvements

For major changes or new content additions, please open an issue first to discuss your proposed changes with the BMTC team.
